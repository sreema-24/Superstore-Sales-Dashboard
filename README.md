# Superstore Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview
A multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 4 years of US retail sales data from the Superstore dataset. Built to uncover revenue trends, regional performance, product profitability, and customer segment insights.

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop (DAX, Power Query, Data Modeling)
- SQL (MySQL)
- Dataset: Superstore Sales Data (Kaggle)

## Dashboard Pages
- **Page 1 - Overview:** KPI cards (Total Sales, Profit, Orders, Profit Margin), Sales by Region, Category and Segment
- **Page 2 - Sales Trends:** Year over Year sales growth, Monthly trend, Sales by Ship Mode
- **Page 3 - Regional Analysis:** Sales and Profit by State (Map), Sales by Region
- **Page 4 - Product Performance:** Top 10 Products by Sales, Profit Treemap, Sub-Category breakdown
- **Page 5 - Customer Segments:** Sales, Profit and Orders by Segment

## Key Insights
- Total Revenue: $2.30M across 5K orders
- Technology is the highest revenue category (36.4%)
- West region leads in total sales
- Consumer segment drives 50.56% of total sales
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
